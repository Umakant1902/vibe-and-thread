Privacy Policy for Vibe & Thread

Last updated: July 2026

At Vibe & Thread, accessible from our application and Pinterest platform, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Vibe & Thread and how we use it.

Information We Collect

Our application interacts directly with the Pinterest API to schedule and publish fashion lookbook content. We do not collect, store, or sell personal user identification data.

Use of Pinterest API

The Pinterest API is utilized strictly for automated publishing of curated imagery and link routing for fashion lookbooks managed under the Vibe & Thread profile.

Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information regarding our Privacy Policy, please reach out via our official profile.
